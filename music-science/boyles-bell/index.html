About Boyle’s Discovery:

In the 1660s, Robert Boyle placed a ringing bell under a glass jar and pumped the air out. As the air thinned, the bell grew quieter even though it kept ringing. Boyle showed that sound needs a medium (air) to travel. With fewer air molecules, there’s less “stuff” to vibrate your eardrum—so loudness drops—while the pitch of the bell (its vibration rate) stays essentially the same.
