About Helmholtz’s Discovery

Hermann von Helmholtz described this experiment in his book On the Sensations of Tone (1863). When two tuning forks are tuned to the same pitch, striking one will cause the other to resonate and even move a light object like a ping-pong ball. This demonstrates sympathetic resonance: vibrations in one object can set another into motion when they share the same natural frequency.
