About Helmholtz’s Demonstration

Hermann von Helmholtz discussed sympathetic vibration in On the Sensations of Tone (1863). A tuning fork responds most strongly to vibrations at its own natural frequency. When two forks match, striking one can make the other vibrate even though it was never touched. The light ping-pong ball makes that otherwise-small motion easier to see.
