About Chladni’s Discovery

In the late eighteenth century, Ernst Chladni sprinkled sand on metal plates and set them vibrating by drawing a violin bow along an edge. At resonant frequencies, the grains bounced away from strongly moving regions and collected along nearly motionless nodal lines. Modern demonstrations often use a speaker or electronic vibration driver so the frequency can be controlled precisely.
