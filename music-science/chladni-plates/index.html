About Chladni’s Discovery

In the late eighteenth century, Ernst Chladni sprinkled sand on metal plates and set them vibrating with a violin bow. At resonant frequencies, the grains bounced away from strongly moving regions and collected along nearly motionless nodal lines. The symmetrical patterns became known as Chladni figures and helped make sound vibration visible.
