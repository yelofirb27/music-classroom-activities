How to Play Notable

Welcome to Notable!
The goal of the game is to find as many words in the time limit as possible.
But there's a twist, rather than the standard alphabet, we're using musical notes.

Gameplay:

A grid of musical notes, representing letters, will appear.
Click on adjacent notes to form a word.
Notes can only be used once per word.
Words must be at least 3 letters long.
Submit your word by clicking the "Submit Word" button.
The game has a timer of three minutes.
All words are composed of A-G.
A musical tone will confirm the word is valid.

Scoring:

A three letter word is worth one point.
For each additional letter, an extra point is granted.
Each valid word also extends the timer by five seconds!
